VIDEO 1 — workspace and case overview

Meet Red Thread, a digital forensics prototype that brings fragmented evidence into one investigation workspace.

The dashboard summarizes case activity, evidence files, event types, and findings that need an investigator's attention.

Opening Operation PhishTrace brings the synthetic case's evidence, events, and findings into one overview.

Investigators can update case status and notes while keeping the investigation's context together.

The audit trail records uploads, verification checks, and case updates for later review.

The evidence vault lists each file, its recorded hash, processing status, and integrity result.



VIDEO 2 — investigation and reporting

From the evidence vault, we can inspect original files and prepare new evidence for processing.

Authentication analysis highlights login activity and suspicious patterns, with parsed events available for closer review.

Behind these views, Red Thread extracts structured events and identifiers from logs, chats, invoices, payment records, and images.

It compares shared identifiers across sources, keeping exact matches separate from conflicts and tentative leads. The core checks work independently of AI.

The relationship graph brings evidence, events, and entities together. Selecting a node reveals its details and connections.

Findings can be filtered by severity, helping investigators focus on amount discrepancies, payee mismatches, and other issues.

Opening a finding reveals its explanation, supporting records, limitations, and alternative explanations. Investigators can record a review decision after checking the evidence.

Back in the vault, we inspect the invoice and payment screenshot directly. SHA-256 verification checks whether stored bytes match their intake hash; it does not establish whether a file was authentic before upload.

The unified timeline places extracted events in sequence, while flagging inferred dates and uncertain timestamps. This helps investigators distinguish recorded facts from assumptions.

The optional AI assistant answers questions using selected case records. Here, we ask it to explain the payment discrepancy.

In this synthetic case, the screenshot shows four thousand five hundred rupees, while the ledger records forty-five thousand under the same transaction reference.

The response includes source citations, uncertainties, and suggested next steps, giving investigators references to verify.

The reporting workspace provides PDF and JSON exports that capture evidence hashes, findings, timeline events, and review information.

The report keeps these details together for review. Findings remain investigative indicators, and the export is a draft requiring human assessment.

Red Thread: connecting evidence, with investigators in control.
